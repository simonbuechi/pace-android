## 6. How to Upload to Google Play Console

1. Log in to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Click **Create app**:
   - App name: `Pace: Interval Workout Timer`
   - Default language: `English (United States)` or `German (Germany)`
   - App or game: `App`
   - Free or paid: `Free`
3. In **App integrity / Play App Signing**, ensure Google Play App Signing is enabled (recommended: "Use Google-generated key" or "Export and upload a key from Java KeyStore").
4. Go to **Testing > Closed testing** or **Production > Releases > Create new release**:
   - Upload the generated bundle file:
     `app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`
   - Release name: `1.0.0 (10)`
   - Release notes:
     ```text
     Initial release of Pace - Minimalist interval and workout timer with custom routines, large fullscreen display, and 20 languages.
     ```
5. Complete the **Main store listing** (copy text and upload `icon-512.png` and `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` from `playstore/`).
6. Complete the **App content** questionnaires (Privacy policy, Ads, Content rating, Target audience, Data safety, Foreground service declaration).
7. Submit the release for review!
